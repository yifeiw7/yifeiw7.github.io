04 — About

Hi, I’m Yifei.

I’m an engineer who cares as much about how technology feels as about how it works.

I study Engineering Science at the University of Toronto, majoring in Machine Intelligence with minors in Engineering Business and Robotics. I’ve shipped real-time ML on factory floors, fine-tuned language and speech models, and I’m now writing a thesis on detecting sea-ice arches from satellite imagery.

Outside of code I paint, design characters and public art, and direct and edit dance films. I think those habits make me a better engineer: they sharpen my eye for detail, rhythm and the moment where something clicks for the person using it. I’m most excited by work where those worlds meet, like creative tools, intelligent products and interfaces that feel human.

I speak English, Mandarin and French.
