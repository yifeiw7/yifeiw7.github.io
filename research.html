My research sits at the edges of computer vision and natural language: finding structure in satellite imagery of a changing Arctic, and letting people ask questions of their data in plain language.

In progress

Undergraduate thesis · Jul 2026 — Present

Ice Arch Detection

Ice arches form where sea ice jams in narrow Arctic channels, and they control how much ice flows south each year. I’m building a computer vision pipeline that automatically detects and localizes these arches in satellite imagery, so their formation and collapse can be tracked across decades of climate data.
